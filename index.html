About

Built from real life

Great North Notes is a small Canadian shop. Every workbook started as a problem in my own life. I built a system, it worked, and now it’s a workbook you can use too.

You won’t find trends or filler here. You’ll find clear steps, plenty of room to write, and no judgment about where you’re starting from.
